Landbase privacy policy

Landbase is a private, internal tool. When you sign in with Google, Landbase receives only your email address. It uses it for one thing: checking that you are on the list of people allowed in.

Landbase does not store your email address or any other information about you, does not share it with anyone, and does not use it for advertising or any other purpose. Google keeps its own sign-in records under Google's privacy policy.

Questions: ask the person who invited you.
